# VANAR: WILD CODE
## Episode 1 — “The Canopy That Never Sleeps”
**Format:** Cinematic 3D animated adventure  
**Target runtime:** 27:00  
**Draft:** Screenplay v0.1 — dialogue and action draft; timing is a production target, not a locked edit.

### Episode purpose
Introduce NeoVan as a thriving contemporary vanar civilization, establish Ayaan's curiosity and impulsiveness, show the trust between Ayaan and his friends, present Veer's responsible leadership, and end with the first concrete sign that someone is mapping the city's defenses.

### Main cast in this episode
- **Ayaan:** curious, athletic, quick to act before thinking.
- **Chiku:** inventor; fast-talking and inventive.
- **Mira:** observant strategist.
- **Kabir:** strong, patient, protective.
- **Zed:** scout; playful but vigilant.
- **Veer:** Ayaan's father and elected community leader.
- **Kairav:** Veer's brother; calm, controlled, politically ambitious.
- **Watcher:** unidentified operative working for Kairav.

## SCENE 1 — MORNING ABOVE THE GREEN
**Time target:** 00:00–05:00  
**Location:** NeoVan canopy transit and residential platforms  
**Purpose:** Establish the city, its culture, and Ayaan's personality.

**FADE IN:**

**EXT. NEO VAN — CANOPY SKYLINE — DAWN**

A sea of green stretches to the horizon. But this is no untouched wilderness. Suspended walkways weave between giant trees. Solar leaves turn toward the morning light. Small electric trams glide silently along living-root rails. Water runs through transparent channels, feeding rooftop gardens.

The city wakes in layers: shutters rise, market lights blink on, birds scatter from a passing courier drone. Vanar residents of different ages cross bridges, exchange food, repair equipment, and greet one another.

A low, warm musical motif begins with hand percussion and forest ambience.

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**  
In NeoVan, the forest isn't behind us. It's the reason we built anything at all.

A courier tram approaches a split in the track. A warning light flashes amber.

On a parallel walkway, AYAAN races a small delivery drone. His teal utility jacket flaps behind him.

**CHIKU (COMMS)**  
Ayaan, reminder: that drone is carrying medicine, not your personal racing trophy.

**AYAAN**  
Then why did you program it to make that annoying victory beep?

**CHIKU (COMMS)**  
For morale. Yours is currently the problem.

Ayaan grins and leaps a narrow gap. The drone takes the safe route and pulls ahead.

**AYAAN**  
That doesn't count. It has a motor.

He catches a hanging maintenance strap, swings around a support beam, and lands on the platform just as an elderly vendor struggles with a jammed cargo cart.

Ayaan's eyes follow the drone for one more beat. Then he turns back and braces the cart.

**VENDOR**  
Your race is getting away.

**AYAAN**  
It'll survive. Your fruit might not.

He lifts the cart's wheel free. Oranges roll across the platform. A child laughs; Ayaan and the vendor scramble to catch them.

Across the way, MIRA watches the scene with folded arms. KABIR quietly catches the last orange before it drops through a gap.

**MIRA**  
You know, one day you might finish a race.

**AYAAN**  
One day you might admit I was winning.

**KABIR**  
You were losing to a delivery drone.

**AYAAN**  
A suspiciously fast delivery drone.

ZED drops down from a higher rail, landing in a crouch.

**ZED**  
I checked. The drone's legal. Your excuses aren't.

They laugh. The camera rises to reveal the city's scale and its careful balance of technology and living forest.

A brief distant flicker crosses a relay tower on the outer boundary. No one notices except Ayaan, whose smile fades for half a second.

**CUT TO TITLE: VANAR: WILD CODE**

## SCENE 2 — A FRIENDLY RACE
**Time target:** 05:00–10:00  
**Location:** Elevated market walkways and community repair stall  
**Purpose:** Build the friend group and plant a small technical clue.

The market is loud and colorful. Food stalls use reusable heat plates. Repair kiosks display open circuit boards. A street musician blends a traditional drum pattern with a compact electronic loop.

Chiku has set up a tiny obstacle course for the delivery drone. It threads between floating markers.

**CHIKU**  
Today's challenge: one clean lap, no collisions, and no illegal use of public infrastructure.

**AYAAN**  
You made that rule after I won last time.

**CHIKU**  
You used a public laundry line as a shortcut.

**AYAAN**  
It was a very efficient line.

Mira studies the course layout.

**MIRA**  
The third marker is drifting. Wind shear from the east canopy.

Chiku checks his handheld screen. The marker's position jumps twice, then settles.

**CHIKU**  
That's not wind. The marker's getting a corrupted location ping.

Ayaan leans over the screen.

**AYAAN**  
Could the tower be glitching again?

**CHIKU**  
The tower doesn't glitch. It has a maintenance schedule.

**MIRA**  
Everything glitches. The question is whether it's random.

Kabir gently nudges Ayaan away from the edge as he leans too far over the railing.

**KABIR**  
You can investigate after you stop trying to fall out of the city.

The friends run the course. Ayaan is fast but overcommits at the drifting marker. Mira takes a wider route, Zed scouts a shortcut, and Kabir helps steady a loose platform panel so no one trips.

Chiku's drone suddenly freezes in midair. Its lights pulse in a repeating pattern: three short flashes, a pause, then two.

**CHIKU**  
That's not my signal.

He resets the controller. The drone drops gently into his hands.

**AYAAN**  
Can you trace it?

**CHIKU**  
Maybe. But first, we do the boring part: report a fault instead of poking at it until it explodes.

Ayaan gives him an exaggerated salute.

**AYAAN**  
I have never poked anything until it exploded.

Everyone stares at him.

**AYAAN**  
Not recently.

A public announcement chimes. Veer's voice carries across the market.

**VEER (P.A.)**  
Residents near the outer boundary: please avoid the old relay walkways until the morning inspection is complete. There is no immediate danger. Updates will follow through the civic channel.

The crowd continues, but a few residents exchange worried looks.

Mira notices a small maintenance sticker on Chiku's drone controller. The printed inspection date has been overwritten by hand.

**MIRA**  
Chiku. Who serviced this?

**CHIKU**  
The civic workshop. Why?

**MIRA**  
Because somebody changed the inspection date.

Ayaan looks toward the distant boundary tower again.

**AYAAN**  
Then let's ask the workshop.

**MIRA**  
We ask. We don't break in.

**AYAAN**  
I said ask.

**ZED**  
Your face said break in.

They head toward the repair stall as the music shifts from playful to curious.

## SCENE 3 — THE LEADER'S BRIEFING
**Time target:** 10:00–15:00  
**Location:** Civic operations hub  
**Purpose:** Establish Veer's leadership style and Kairav's political pressure.

**INT. CIVIC OPERATIONS HUB — MORNING**

The operations hub sits inside a broad living-tree structure reinforced with sustainable composite beams. Transparent displays show transit flow, water levels, forest fire sensors, and public service requests.

Veer stands with a small team of engineers. No throne, no ceremonial armor: only a field coat, a tablet, and a city full of people depending on him.

**ENGINEER**  
Three relay stations reported short signal interruptions. No equipment failure is visible in the logs.

**VEER**  
Then don't label it equipment failure yet. Keep the affected routes open where safe, put people at the physical checkpoints, and tell residents what we know. Don't fill the gaps with guesses.

A door opens. KAIRAV enters with two aides. His black modern coat is immaculate; his voice is smooth.

**KAIRAV**  
You are treating a coordinated event like a loose cable.

**VEER**  
I'm treating incomplete evidence like incomplete evidence.

**KAIRAV**  
People want to know that someone is in control.

**VEER**  
They deserve to know what is happening. That isn't the same thing.

Kairav glances at the display.

**KAIRAV**  
The outer relays map every approach to the city. If someone is testing them, we should restrict access immediately.

**VEER**  
Temporarily reroute maintenance crews. Don't lock down neighborhoods based on a signal pattern.

**KAIRAV**  
And if the next interruption opens a gate?

**VEER**  
Then we respond to the evidence we have. We don't use fear to collect power we may not need.

The room falls quiet. Kairav keeps his expression neutral.

**KAIRAV**  
You always did believe trust was a security system.

**VEER**  
It is one. Not the only one.

Kairav steps closer, lowering his voice.

**KAIRAV**  
Your son has been seen around the old relay routes.

Veer's face tightens, but he doesn't react impulsively.

**VEER**  
Ayaan helps people. If he found something relevant, I'll hear it from him.

**KAIRAV**  
Of course. Family first.

**VEER**  
The city first. Family included.

Kairav turns to leave. At the doorway, he pauses.

**KAIRAV**  
I hope your confidence is justified, brother.

After he exits, the engineer speaks quietly.

**ENGINEER**  
Should I flag Ayaan's access?

**VEER**  
No. If he has information, I want him to bring it to us without being treated like a suspect. Increase physical checks at the old relay. Quietly.

Veer turns back to the display. A small section of the outer network blinks out, then returns.

His expression says he knows the problem is bigger than a broken sensor.

## SCENE 4 — A SIGNAL IN THE ROOTS
**Time target:** 15:00–22:00  
**Location:** Abandoned relay station at the forest boundary  
**Purpose:** Ayaan finds proof of deliberate mapping and faces a consequence for taking risks.

**EXT. OLD RELAY WALKWAY — AFTERNOON**

The old relay station is half reclaimed by vines. Its solar panels are dusty; the service bridge hums underfoot. A warning sign reads: ACCESS RESTRICTED DURING INSPECTION.

Ayaan, Chiku, and Mira approach from a public trail.

**MIRA**  
We are looking from outside. If the door is sealed, we leave.

**AYAAN**  
Agreed.

**CHIKU**  
I'm recording that. Timestamp and everything.

Ayaan reaches the door. It is already ajar.

He stops. The joke disappears from his face.

**AYAAN**  
That wasn't open on the city map.

Mira signals them to stay behind her. They enter slowly.

**INT. OLD RELAY STATION — CONTINUOUS**

Dust hangs in shafts of green light. A service console is powered, though the station should be offline. Chiku connects a passive diagnostic reader without changing any settings.

On screen: a series of short access bursts, each aimed at a different relay. A map slowly forms—not of the city itself, but of its response times and blind spots.

**CHIKU**  
Someone isn't trying to shut the network down. They're measuring how fast it recovers.

**MIRA**  
A rehearsal.

Ayaan spots a small device attached beneath the console. Its casing bears no civic workshop mark.

**AYAAN**  
Can you copy the log?

**CHIKU**  
Read-only copy. Then we go.

The device emits the same pattern as the drone: three short pulses, a pause, two pulses.

Chiku's reader begins to transfer the file. A warning appears: REMOTE SESSION ACTIVE.

**MIRA**  
Someone is connected right now.

Ayaan reaches for the device.

**MIRA**  
Don't touch it!

Too late. Ayaan pulls his hand back as a tiny indicator turns red. The station's lights snap on.

A distant motor starts. Through the window, a maintenance drone changes direction and heads toward the relay.

**CHIKU**  
It knows we're here.

**AYAAN**  
Can you finish the copy?

**CHIKU**  
Eight seconds!

The station door locks. Mira grabs a manual release lever, but it doesn't move.

**MIRA**  
Ayaan, find the emergency latch. Chiku, kill the reader when the copy finishes.

Ayaan races along the wall, finds a rusted release panel, and forces it open. Inside, wires have been cut and rejoined in a new configuration.

**AYAAN**  
This was modified recently.

**MIRA**  
Can you open it?

**AYAAN**  
Working on it!

Chiku's reader hits 100 percent. He disconnects it.

**CHIKU**  
Copy's done!

Ayaan bridges two safe low-voltage contacts with the insulated tool from his jacket. The magnetic lock releases. The three spill onto the service bridge as the maintenance drone rounds the corner.

It does not fire. It scans them, flashes a white light, and transmits.

Mira throws a loose tarp over the reader's glowing screen. They duck down the stairs into the trees.

**EXT. FOREST SERVICE PATH — MOMENTS LATER**

They stop beneath thick roots, breathing hard.

**MIRA**  
We said look from outside.

**AYAAN**  
I know.

**MIRA**  
You didn't just risk yourself. You risked all of us.

Ayaan can't argue.

Chiku checks the copied data. Most of the log is encrypted, but a partial route identifier remains.

**CHIKU**  
This is enough to prove the signal was deliberate. Not enough to tell us who did it.

Ayaan looks back toward the relay station.

**AYAAN**  
Then we take it to my father.

Mira nods, still angry, but relieved.

Above them, unseen in the canopy, a small lens retracts into a dark maintenance casing.

## SCENE 5 — EYES IN THE DARK
**Time target:** 22:00–27:00  
**Location:** Rain-soaked service bridge and an undisclosed control room  
**Purpose:** Close the episode with a credible threat and a strong hook.

**EXT. SERVICE BRIDGE — DUSK**

Rain begins to fall. Ayaan and his friends move toward the public transit route. Chiku has secured the data copy inside an insulated case.

Ayaan walks a little behind the others.

**AYAAN**  
Mira was right. I rushed in.

**MIRA**  
You did.

**AYAAN**  
You could pretend to make me feel better.

**MIRA**  
I could. But then you'd do it again.

Kabir slows so Ayaan can catch up.

**KABIR**  
You saw a problem and wanted to fix it. That's not the bad part.

**AYAAN**  
What's the bad part?

**KABIR**  
Deciding that wanting to help means you get to choose the risk for everyone else.

Ayaan absorbs that. He looks at the data case in Chiku's hands.

**AYAAN**  
Next time, we make the plan together.

**ZED**  
Good. Because next time, I'd like a plan that includes dry socks.

They share a small laugh. The tension eases, but only for a moment.

A public transit screen flickers. For a fraction of a second, the city map displays the old relay station's access path. Then the screen returns to normal.

Mira sees it.

**MIRA**  
Did anyone else—

The lights go out along the bridge. Emergency strips activate, painting the rain red.

Across the gap, a silhouette stands beneath a maintenance canopy. Face hidden. Still as a statue.

Ayaan steps forward, but Mira catches his sleeve.

**MIRA**  
Together. Remember?

The silhouette turns and disappears into the rain.

**CUT TO:**

**INT. UNDISCLOSED CONTROL ROOM — NIGHT**

A dark room filled with quiet displays. The copied signal pattern appears on one screen. A second screen shows Ayaan and his friends leaving the relay station.

A WATCHER removes a small receiver from their glove.

**WATCHER**  
The boy found the probe.

A voice answers through an encrypted channel. Calm. Familiar.

**KAIRAV (V.O.)**  
Did he see the route map?

**WATCHER**  
Enough to ask questions. Not enough to identify the source.

A pause.

**KAIRAV (V.O.)**  
Then let him ask. Curiosity can be useful. It can also lead someone exactly where we need him.

The Watcher waits.

**WATCHER**  
And the leader?

Kairav studies a live image of Veer standing alone in the operations hub.

**KAIRAV (V.O.)**  
My brother still believes the city will choose trust over fear.

The display shifts to a map of NeoVan's outer defenses. Several small sections glow amber.

**KAIRAV (V.O.)**  
Let's find out what it chooses when the lights go out.

One by one, the amber markers turn red.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

A final low percussion hit. Forest ambience returns—then a faint relay pulse repeats: three short, pause, two.

**END OF EPISODE 1.**

## Production notes
- **Timing:** The five blocks total 27 minutes, but the first animatic must validate pacing. Expand or trim pauses, travel, and action only after a timed storyboard pass.
- **Dialogue:** Record scratch dialogue before animation; avoid locking expensive shots before the edit works.
- **Continuity:** The repeating three-pulse/pause/two-pulse signal appears in the drone and relay. Keep it identical in later episodes.
- **Visual restraint:** The city should feel lived-in and contemporary. Avoid palace imagery, unexplained magical technology, or generic medieval costumes.
- **Next episode hook:** The friends take the copied relay log to Veer; Kairav uses public concern to push for expanded security powers.
