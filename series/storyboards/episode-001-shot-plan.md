# VANAR: WILD CODE
## Episode 1 — Shot Plan & Animatic Blueprint
**File:** `series/storyboards/episode-001-shot-plan.md`  
**Revision:** v0.1  
**Target runtime:** 27:00  
**Status:** Production blueprint only — no rendered shots or final timing are implied.

This plan translates the Episode 1 screenplay into a first-pass visual/animatic plan. The timings below are **editorial allocations**, not verified shot lengths. Dialogue, staging, and transitions must be timed in an animatic before animation begins.

## Global visual rules

- **World:** NeoVan is a contemporary forest city: living trees integrated with transit, civic technology, workshops, renewable power, and public spaces. No palace/maharaja visual language.
- **Rendering direction:** cinematic stylized-realistic 3D; believable materials, expressive faces, readable silhouettes, controlled depth of field, physically motivated lighting.
- **Character continuity:** use the exact visual locks in `series/characters.json`. Do not redesign characters per shot.
- **Camera:** motivated movement; readable geography; avoid constant orbiting and excessive slow motion.
- **Tech motif:** the hostile signal is always **three short pulses, a pause, two short pulses**. Keep its rhythm, color, and interface language consistent.
- **Safety of the story:** Ayaan is brave but impulsive; consequences are real. The mentor/devotional arc is not part of this episode and must not be foreshadowed with disrespectful gimmicks.
- **Prompting:** prompts below are starting points for image/shot generation, not a guarantee of character consistency. Use approved character reference images and seed/reference controls where supported.

## Sequence 1 — Morning Above the Green (00:00–05:00)

### S01 — NeoVan wakes
- **Time allocation:** 00:00–00:55
- **Shot design:** Begin on dark green canopy silhouettes. Dawn light reveals layers of suspended walkways, root-integrated rail lines, water channels, solar leaves, homes, and commuters. End with a tram moving across frame.
- **Camera:** Slow aerial crane/push; establish clear city geography before cutting closer.
- **Image prompt:** “Wide cinematic establishing shot of NeoVan, a thriving contemporary vanar civilization built through giant rainforest canopy, suspended pedestrian bridges, living-root electric tram rails, transparent water channels, rooftop gardens, subtle solar technology, dawn mist and warm sun shafts, densely inhabited and optimistic, high-end stylized realistic 3D animation, coherent architecture, no palace, no medieval costumes, 16:9.”
- **Sound:** Forest dawn ambience, distant civic activity, tram hum; warm percussion motif begins.
- **Dialogue:** Narrator: “In NeoVan, the forest isn't behind us. It's the reason we built anything at all.”
- **Continuity:** Establish the same skyline and outer relay tower for later returns.

### S02 — The amber warning
- **Time allocation:** 00:55–01:30
- **Shot design:** A courier tram approaches a track split; an amber warning blinks once, then returns to normal. Background commuters continue.
- **Camera:** Medium telephoto tracking shot, then insert of the warning indicator.
- **Prompt:** “Modern compact electric courier tram gliding on living-root rails between giant canopy platforms, a small amber signal briefly flashing at a junction, morning mist, believable safety hardware, cinematic 3D animation, restrained tension, 16:9.”
- **Sound:** Rail hum with one subtle electronic chirp; do not make the warning feel catastrophic yet.
- **Dialogue:** None.

### S03 — Ayaan races the drone
- **Time allocation:** 01:30–02:30
- **Shot design:** Introduce Ayaan on a parallel walkway racing a medicine drone. Show his teal jacket and left-ear notch in a readable medium shot; cut to a wide jump over a narrow gap.
- **Camera:** Side-tracking shot into a wide action shot; keep landing geography readable.
- **Prompt:** “Ayaan, athletic young vanar with amber-brown fur, small notch in left ear and teal utility jacket, racing a compact medicine delivery drone along an elevated forest-city walkway, playful confidence, wind moving jacket, cinematic stylized-realistic 3D, clear silhouette, no costume changes, 16:9.”
- **Sound/dialogue:** Chiku on comms: “Ayaan, reminder: that drone is carrying medicine, not your personal racing trophy.” Ayaan: “Then why did you program it to make that annoying victory beep?”
- **Animation note:** Ayaan's movement should feel athletic, not superhuman.

### S04 — Choosing to help
- **Time allocation:** 02:30–03:35
- **Shot design:** Drone pulls ahead. Ayaan turns back to help an elderly vendor free a jammed cargo cart; oranges roll. He catches one just before it drops through a gap.
- **Camera:** Follow the drone briefly, then rack focus to the vendor; low close-up on the orange catch; medium two-shot with vendor.
- **Prompt:** “Young amber-brown-furred vanar in teal utility jacket abandoning a race to help an elderly vendor with a jammed cargo cart on a busy canopy walkway, oranges rolling, a child laughing, compassionate spontaneous action, lived-in futuristic forest city, expressive 3D animation, 16:9.”
- **Sound/dialogue:** Vendor: “Your race is getting away.” Ayaan: “It'll survive. Your fruit might not.”
- **Performance:** This is the first proof that compassion outranks ego, even if Ayaan still jokes.

### S05 — The friends and distant flicker
- **Time allocation:** 03:35–05:00
- **Shot design:** Mira observes, Kabir catches the last orange, Zed drops from a rail. Banter establishes group chemistry. Camera rises; a relay tower flickers in the far distance. Ayaan notices for half a beat. Title card.
- **Camera:** Intimate group coverage, then rising wide; hold the relay flicker subtly in the background.
- **Prompt:** “Four young vanar friends on a busy elevated market walkway in NeoVan: Ayaan in teal jacket, observant russet-furred Mira in blue-grey streetwear, large grey-brown Kabir with forearm guards, lean sandy-furred Zed with orange scarf; playful banter, then a distant relay tower flickers beyond the canopy, cinematic 3D, 16:9.”
- **Sound/dialogue:** Mira: “You know, one day you might finish a race.” Ayaan: “One day you might admit I was winning.” Kabir: “You were losing to a delivery drone.” Zed: “I checked. The drone's legal. Your excuses aren't.”
- **Edit:** Title “VANAR: WILD CODE” after the distant flicker; avoid a long title hold that breaks the 5-minute allocation.

## Sequence 2 — A Friendly Race (05:00–10:00)

### S06 — Market life and obstacle course
- **Time allocation:** 05:00–06:00
- **Shot design:** Market stalls, repair kiosks, reusable heat plates, street musician; Chiku presents a small drone obstacle course.
- **Camera:** Lateral market glide into a medium shot of Chiku and the drone.
- **Prompt:** “Lively contemporary canopy market in NeoVan, repair kiosks with open circuit boards, food stalls with reusable heat plates, street musician mixing hand drums with electronic loop, compact golden-brown vanar inventor Chiku wearing round protective goggles and modular tool belt setting up a drone obstacle course, stylized realistic 3D, 16:9.”
- **Sound:** Market layers and diegetic rhythm; keep dialogue intelligible.

### S07 — Course rules and character beats
- **Time allocation:** 06:00–07:00
- **Shot design:** Chiku explains the rules; Ayaan jokes about the laundry-line shortcut. Mira studies marker placement while Kabir keeps Ayaan away from the edge.
- **Camera:** Shot/reverse-shot with short reaction inserts; no unnecessary camera spins.
- **Dialogue:** Chiku: “Today's challenge: one clean lap, no collisions, and no illegal use of public infrastructure.” Ayaan: “You made that rule after I won last time.” Chiku: “You used a public laundry line as a shortcut.”
- **Sound:** Playful percussion, small drone motors.

### S08 — Marker drift
- **Time allocation:** 07:00–08:00
- **Shot design:** Third marker drifts. Mira calls out wind shear; Chiku sees the location ping jump unnaturally twice.
- **Camera:** Over-shoulder on controller, macro insert on the jittering location trace, reaction close-ups.
- **Prompt:** “Close-up of a compact handheld drone controller showing a location marker jumping twice on a clean technical interface, reflected in Chiku's round goggles, market colors softly blurred behind, cinematic 3D insert, legible simple UI, no random text.”
- **Sound:** The market bed dips; two quiet digital ticks.
- **Dialogue:** Mira: “The third marker is drifting. Wind shear from the east canopy.” Chiku: “That's not wind. The marker's getting a corrupted location ping.”

### S09 — The five-pulse signature
- **Time allocation:** 08:00–08:45
- **Shot design:** Drone freezes in midair. Its lights pulse three times, pause, then twice. Chiku catches it safely.
- **Camera:** Stable medium-wide for the freeze, then close-up of the pulse pattern.
- **Sound:** Exact signature: three short tones, short silence, two short tones. Reuse identical pattern in the relay and final sting.
- **Dialogue:** Chiku: “That's not my signal.” Ayaan: “Can you trace it?” Chiku: “Maybe. But first, we do the boring part: report a fault instead of poking at it until it explodes.”
- **Technical note:** Save this sound as a reusable audio asset later; do not recreate the rhythm inconsistently.

### S10 — Official warning and altered sticker
- **Time allocation:** 08:45–10:00
- **Shot design:** Veer's public announcement requests residents avoid old relay walkways. Mira spots a handwritten alteration to the controller's maintenance sticker. Friends decide to ask the workshop.
- **Camera:** Wide market listening reaction, close-up on sticker, group medium shot looking toward boundary tower.
- **Dialogue:** Veer (P.A.): “Residents near the outer boundary: please avoid the old relay walkways until the morning inspection is complete. There is no immediate danger. Updates will follow through the civic channel.” Mira: “Because somebody changed the inspection date.” Ayaan: “Then let's ask the workshop.” Mira: “We ask. We don't break in.”
- **Sound:** Market continues naturally; music turns curious, not frightening.

## Sequence 3 — The Leader's Briefing (10:00–15:00)

### S11 — Civic operations hub
- **Time allocation:** 10:00–10:50
- **Shot design:** Reveal transit, water, fire-sensor, and public-service dashboards inside a reinforced living-tree civic structure. Veer listens to engineers.
- **Camera:** Slow interior reveal, then balanced two-shots; the room should feel functional, not royal.
- **Prompt:** “Contemporary civic operations hub built inside a vast living tree, sustainable composite beams, transparent displays of transit flow, water levels and forest sensors, Veer a broad-shouldered older vanar with salt-and-pepper facial fur and charcoal field coat with copper trim, listening carefully to engineers, cinematic 3D, no throne or royal decor, 16:9.”
- **Dialogue:** Engineer reports three relay interruptions without visible equipment failure.
- **Sound:** Low room tone, quiet interface pings.

### S12 — Veer's response
- **Time allocation:** 10:50–11:40
- **Shot design:** Veer gives measured instructions: keep safe routes open, put staff at checkpoints, communicate known facts.
- **Camera:** Medium close-up on Veer; reaction insert on engineer accepting the plan.
- **Dialogue:** Veer: “Then don't label it equipment failure yet. Keep the affected routes open where safe, put people at the physical checkpoints, and tell residents what we know. Don't fill the gaps with guesses.”
- **Performance:** Authority through clarity and care, never shouting.

### S13 — Kairav enters
- **Time allocation:** 11:40–12:20
- **Shot design:** Kairav enters with two aides; immaculate black modern coat, muted crimson accents, pale scar over right brow. His entrance shifts the room's social temperature.
- **Camera:** Doorway silhouette into a controlled push-in; maintain Veer's eyeline.
- **Prompt:** “Tall dark-furred vanar Kairav with pale scar over right brow, black contemporary armored coat with muted crimson accents, entering a practical forest-city operations room with two aides, calm controlled expression, cinematic 3D, restrained menace, no crown, 16:9.”
- **Sound:** Room tone narrows slightly; no villain sting.

### S14 — Debate: safety versus fear
- **Time allocation:** 12:20–14:10
- **Shot design:** Cross-cut the brothers as Kairav argues for restriction and Veer refuses to turn incomplete evidence into control. Let silence and eye contact carry the tension.
- **Camera:** Alternating medium close-ups; occasional two-shot to show they share the same room but not the same philosophy.
- **Dialogue anchors:** Kairav: “People want to know that someone is in control.” Veer: “They deserve to know what is happening. That isn't the same thing.” Kairav: “You always did believe trust was a security system.” Veer: “It is one. Not the only one.”
- **Sound:** Minimal low strings, almost imperceptible.

### S15 — Ayaan used as pressure
- **Time allocation:** 14:10–15:00
- **Shot design:** Kairav mentions Ayaan near old relay routes. Veer remains composed; after Kairav exits, Veer quietly orders physical checks increased. A network section blinks out and returns.
- **Camera:** Close-up on Veer's restrained reaction, Kairav in doorway, final insert on network map.
- **Dialogue:** Kairav: “Your son has been seen around the old relay routes.” Veer: “The city first. Family included.” Veer to engineer: “Increase physical checks at the old relay. Quietly.”
- **Edit:** End on the network blink, then cut to the old relay in daylight.

## Sequence 4 — A Signal in the Roots (15:00–22:00)

### S16 — Approach under warning signs
- **Time allocation:** 15:00–15:50
- **Shot design:** Establish vine-reclaimed relay, dusty solar panels, humming service bridge, restricted-access sign. The friends approach from public trail.
- **Camera:** Wide exterior, then low tracking shot along bridge structure.
- **Prompt:** “Abandoned but recently powered relay station at the edge of a lush contemporary forest city, vines reclaiming composite supports, dusty solar panels, narrow humming service bridge, clear restricted-access warning sign, late afternoon green light, cinematic 3D suspense, 16:9.”
- **Dialogue:** Mira sets boundary: look from outside; leave if sealed. Ayaan agrees.
- **Sound:** Wind through vines, subtle bridge vibration.

### S17 — The door is already open
- **Time allocation:** 15:50–16:35
- **Shot design:** Ayaan reaches the door and notices it is ajar. His joking expression vanishes. Mira signals the others to hold.
- **Camera:** Hand-level shot on the door gap; slow push toward Ayaan's eyes; no jump scare.
- **Dialogue:** Ayaan: “That wasn't open on the city map.”
- **Sound:** Ambient birds fall away gradually, not all at once.

### S18 — The map of response times
- **Time allocation:** 16:35–17:45
- **Shot design:** Inside, dust in green shafts of light. Chiku attaches a passive reader. A map builds from access bursts and response times.
- **Camera:** Slow reveal from console to reflected map in characters' eyes; close-up on the reader.
- **Prompt:** “Interior of a disused forest-city relay station, dust suspended in green sun shafts, a small console still powered, compact diagnostic reader connected in read-only mode, a simple holographic network diagram showing relay response intervals and blind spots, three vanar friends tense and alert, cinematic 3D, 16:9.”
- **Dialogue:** Chiku: “Someone isn't trying to shut the network down. They're measuring how fast it recovers.” Mira: “A rehearsal.”
- **UI rule:** Display an abstract map, not paragraphs of gibberish.

### S19 — Unmarked probe
- **Time allocation:** 17:45–18:30
- **Shot design:** Ayaan spots a small unmarked device under the console. Chiku begins a read-only copy. Signature pulses play.
- **Camera:** Low insert under console; cut to Chiku's progress indicator and the device's pulse light.
- **Sound:** Exact three-short/pause/two-short signal.
- **Dialogue:** Ayaan: “Can you copy the log?” Chiku: “Read-only copy. Then we go.”

### S20 — Remote session active
- **Time allocation:** 18:30–19:15
- **Shot design:** Warning appears: REMOTE SESSION ACTIVE. Mira warns Ayaan not to touch the device; he reaches and triggers the system.
- **Camera:** Tight inserts, fast but readable reaction cuts; red indicator illuminates Ayaan's fingers.
- **Dialogue:** Mira: “Someone is connected right now.” Mira: “Don't touch it!”
- **Sound:** One relay click, then the room's power snaps on. Avoid a loud explosion effect.

### S21 — Station locks down
- **Time allocation:** 19:15–20:15
- **Shot design:** Door locks; maintenance drone turns toward the station. Mira works the manual release, Ayaan finds the tampered latch panel, Chiku waits for copy completion.
- **Camera:** Wide master first to preserve geography, then action inserts. Keep the drone visible through the window.
- **Dialogue:** Chiku: “It knows we're here.” Ayaan: “Can you finish the copy?” Chiku: “Eight seconds!” Mira: “Ayaan, find the emergency latch. Chiku, kill the reader when the copy finishes.”
- **Sound:** Motor approaching, lock clunk, rising pulse under dialogue.

### S22 — Escape and consequence
- **Time allocation:** 20:15–21:20
- **Shot design:** Chiku reaches 100%; Ayaan uses an insulated tool on safe low-voltage contacts. Lock releases; trio escapes. Drone scans and transmits without firing.
- **Camera:** Medium action coverage, wide exit shot, brief scan POV. Avoid making Ayaan look invulnerable.
- **Dialogue:** Ayaan: “This was modified recently.” Mira: “Can you open it?” Ayaan: “Working on it!” Chiku: “Copy's done!”
- **Sound:** Lock disengage, drone servo, leaves disturbed as they flee.

### S23 — The argument beneath the roots
- **Time allocation:** 21:20–22:00
- **Shot design:** Under thick roots, Mira confronts Ayaan. Chiku confirms the copy proves deliberate interference but not the culprit. A lens retracts unseen above them.
- **Camera:** Grounded three-shot, restrained close-ups; final rack focus to the hidden lens.
- **Dialogue:** Mira: “You didn't just risk yourself. You risked all of us.” Chiku: “This is enough to prove the signal was deliberate. Not enough to tell us who did it.” Ayaan: “Then we take it to my father.”
- **Continuity:** Preserve the encrypted log and partial route identifier as evidence for Episode 2.

## Sequence 5 — Eyes in the Dark (22:00–27:00)

### S24 — Rain and accountability
- **Time allocation:** 22:00–23:15
- **Shot design:** Rain begins on the service bridge. Ayaan admits Mira was right; Kabir explains the real mistake was choosing everyone's risk for them. The group makes up without instantly erasing the issue.
- **Camera:** Medium walking master, rain-lit close-ups, group composition that gradually brings Ayaan back into formation.
- **Dialogue:** Ayaan: “Mira was right. I rushed in.” Mira: “You did.” Kabir: “You saw a problem and wanted to fix it. That's not the bad part.” Ayaan: “What's the bad part?” Kabir: “Deciding that wanting to help means you get to choose the risk for everyone else.”
- **Sound:** Rain, bridge resonance, warm motif returns quietly.

### S25 — The city map flickers
- **Time allocation:** 23:15–24:00
- **Shot design:** Transit screen briefly displays the relay access path, then normalizes. Mira notices first. Lights across the bridge fail.
- **Camera:** Insert on public display; pan to Mira's reaction; wide view of the bridge going dark.
- **Dialogue:** Mira: “Did anyone else—”
- **Sound:** Electrical hum cuts; emergency strips activate in muted red. No musical jump sting.

### S26 — The silhouette
- **Time allocation:** 24:00–24:45
- **Shot design:** Across the gap, a still silhouette stands under a maintenance canopy. Ayaan steps forward; Mira catches his sleeve. The figure vanishes into rain.
- **Camera:** Long lens silhouette, over-shoulder group shot, short controlled pan into empty rain.
- **Dialogue:** Mira: “Together. Remember?”
- **Sound:** Rain dominates; one distant metallic footstep, then absence.

### S27 — Watcher reports
- **Time allocation:** 24:45–25:45
- **Shot design:** Cut to an undisclosed control room. Watcher removes receiver; multiple screens show the probe, the route map, and the friends leaving.
- **Camera:** Slow reveal of screens before revealing Watcher; keep the operative's identity hidden.
- **Prompt:** “Dark understated surveillance control room with several clean technical displays, anonymous operative in practical dark gloves removing a small receiver, one display shows a forest-city relay map with amber markers, cinematic realistic 3D lighting, restrained espionage mood, no readable random text, 16:9.”
- **Dialogue:** Watcher: “The boy found the probe.” Kairav (V.O.): “Did he see the route map?” Watcher: “Enough to ask questions. Not enough to identify the source.”
- **Sound:** Soft electronics and room tone.

### S28 — Kairav's final move
- **Time allocation:** 25:45–26:40
- **Shot design:** Kairav studies a live image of Veer at the operations hub. The outer defense map shifts from amber to red marker by marker.
- **Camera:** Tight profile with brow scar visible, slow push to screen reflection, then map insert.
- **Dialogue:** Kairav (V.O.): “Then let him ask. Curiosity can be useful. It can also lead someone exactly where we need him.” Watcher: “And the leader?” Kairav (V.O.): “My brother still believes the city will choose trust over fear. Let's find out what it chooses when the lights go out.”
- **Sound:** Low percussion slowly accumulates; no melodramatic laughter.

### S29 — Signal sting
- **Time allocation:** 26:40–27:00
- **Shot design:** Cut to black. Forest ambience returns, then the five-pulse signal.
- **Sound:** Three short pulses, pause, two short pulses; one low percussion hit after the pattern.
- **End card:** “END OF EPISODE 1.”
- **Next-episode continuity:** Friends bring the copied relay log to Veer; Kairav leverages public concern to push expanded security powers.
