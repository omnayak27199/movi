# Opening theme — direction

**Working title:** “Rise Through the Canopy”  
**Mood:** adventurous, hopeful, percussive, modern; devotional inspiration handled respectfully.  
**Length target:** 60–80 seconds for a full opening; create a shorter version for recaps when needed.

## Original lyric sketch

Through the roots, through the rain,  
We find our way and rise again.  
Hand to hand, we guard the flame,  
A forest strong is more than a name.

When the night hides every road,  
We share the strength to lift the load.  
Heart awake, the dawn will show,  
Together is the way we grow.

## Arrangement notes

- Begin with forest ambience and a restrained hand-percussion pulse.
- Introduce modern low drums and warm bass, then a memorable melodic motif.
- Add layered voices on the final refrain; keep lyrics clear.
- Avoid copying an existing film, song, melody, or signature arrangement.
- Produce stems (rhythm, melody, vocals, ambience) separately for edit flexibility.
