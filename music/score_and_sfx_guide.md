# Score and sound-effects guide

## Sonic identity
NeoVan should sound alive: canopy wind, distant birds, water systems, soft transit motors, workshop tools, and layered public-space ambience. Technology should feel integrated into the forest rather than pasted on top of it.

## Motifs
- **Ayaan:** a curious rising three-note phrase; gradually becomes rhythmically confident.
- **Veer:** warm low strings and restrained percussion; strength without bombast.
- **Kairav:** sparse pulses, controlled sub-bass, and deliberate silence; avoid generic villain laughter.
- **The Wayfarer:** simple breath, soft natural ambience, and a calm melodic interval; never use sacred presence as a cheap jump scare.
- **The community:** interlocking rhythmic patterns that sound strongest when characters cooperate.

## Mix and delivery
- Keep dialogue intelligible and centered.
- Use separate dialogue, music, ambience, and effects stems.
- Avoid clipping; leave headroom for final mastering.
- Check loudness and platform requirements before release.
- Record provenance and license status for every music and SFX asset.
